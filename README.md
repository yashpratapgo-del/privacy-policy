# Privacy Policy for Oros

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

Oros ("the app", "we", "us") is a mountain guessing game made by Deependra Pratap Chand, based in India. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what choices you have.

If you have any questions, contact us at yashpratapgo@gmail.com.

---

## 1. Information we collect

**We do not ask you for any personal information.** There is no sign-up, no login and no account. You do not need to give us your name, email address or phone number to play.

### Information stored only on your device
- **Game data:** Your scores, progress and settings are saved **only on your phone**. We do not receive or store this data on any server.

### Information collected by our advertising partner
- **Advertising data:** The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect your device's **advertising ID**, IP address, approximate location (based on IP address), device information (such as model and operating system), and information about how you interact with ads. See section 3.

We do **not** collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

## 2. How information is used
- Game data on your device is used only to run the game and remember your progress.
- Advertising data is used by Google to show ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud. Ads keep the game free.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
Oros uses Google AdMob to show banner, full-screen and rewarded ads. Google may use cookies and your device's advertising ID to show ads and measure how they perform.

- You can learn how Google uses data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on your device).
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings → Privacy options** inside the app.

## 4. Who we share information with
We do not share any information ourselves, because we do not collect any. The only third party that receives data from the app is **Google AdMob**, as described above.

## 5. How long information is kept
- Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Your rights and choices
You can:
- **Delete all your game data** at any time by clearing the app's storage (**Settings → Apps → Oros → Storage → Clear data**) or by uninstalling the app
- Turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings
- Change your ad consent choice in the app at **Settings → Privacy options** (for users in Europe)
- Contact us with any privacy question or complaint at yashpratapgo@gmail.com

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Security
Because your game data stays on your device and we do not run any servers that store personal information, there is very little data that could be exposed. Data sent to Google AdMob is sent over encrypted connections.

## 8. Children's privacy
Oros is meant for users **aged 13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app and has had personal information collected, contact us at yashpratapgo@gmail.com.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features such as online play or accounts. When we do, we will change the "Effective date" at the top of this page.
